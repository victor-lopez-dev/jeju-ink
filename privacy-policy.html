Privacy Policy

Jeju Ink

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Jeju Ink ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, submit a tattoo inquiry, or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a tattoo inquiry or contact us, we may collect the following information:

Personal and Contact Information

Your name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact method.

Tattoo Inquiry Information

Tattoo descriptions, preferred styles, sizes, placement, reference images, estimated budgets, preferred appointment availability, and any additional information you voluntarily submit.

Website and Technical Information

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, we may collect IP addresses, browser types, device information, website activity, and cookie preferences.

Important: Please avoid submitting sensitive medical information through our general inquiry form unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to review and respond to tattoo inquiries, discuss tattoo concepts, pricing, consultations, and availability, and arrange appointments.

We may also use your information to notify authorized Jeju Ink personnel of new inquiries, maintain business records, improve website functionality and security, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Submitting an inquiry does not automatically enroll you in promotional SMS or email marketing.

3. Internal SMS Notifications

Jeju Ink offers optional SMS communications to customers who expressly consent to receive text messages through our website inquiry forms.

By selecting the optional SMS consent checkbox when submitting a tattoo inquiry, you agree to receive automated text messages from Jeju Ink regarding your tattoo inquiry, submission confirmation, consultation requests, appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders, and related customer service communications.

Message frequency varies depending on your inquiry and appointment activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. After opting out, you will no longer receive messages through the SMS program unless you opt in again.

For assistance, reply HELP to any message or contact us at jeju.inked@gmail.com .

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing tattoo services or submitting an inquiry. You may submit a tattoo inquiry without agreeing to receive text messages.

We do not send promotional or marketing text messages under this inquiry and appointment notification program. Any future marketing SMS program will require appropriate separate consent.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Mobile Information and Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected through our website.

We do not sell or share SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, if collected, are excluded from any other data-sharing categories described in this Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information to authorized service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, process tattoo inquiries, provide requested services, or deliver internal business notifications.

These service providers are not authorized to use customer information for their independent marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

Our website may use third-party technologies to manage inquiries, deliver communications, and support website operations.

FormDesigner

FormDesigner may be used to receive, process, and manage tattoo inquiry submissions.

Twilio

Twilio may be used to deliver authorized internal SMS notifications when new inquiries are submitted.

Website Hosting

Our website hosting provider processes information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the website.

CookieYes

CookieYes may be used to manage cookie consent preferences and related website tracking controls.

Analytics and Advertising Services

If enabled, analytics and advertising services may be used to measure website performance or advertising effectiveness, subject to applicable consent requirements.

These providers may process information according to relevant service agreements and their own privacy practices.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, improve website performance, and understand visitor activity.

Where required by applicable law, optional analytics and advertising technologies will be activated only after appropriate consent has been obtained.

You may manage eligible cookie categories through our cookie consent banner and reopen your preferences using the Cookie Settings link available on our website.

You may also manage certain cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, and disclosure.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Retention

We retain inquiry information and associated communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and fulfill applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer needed will be deleted, anonymized, or otherwise appropriately disposed of, subject to applicable retention requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information we maintain about you.

To submit a privacy request, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

10. Reference Images and Tattoo Artwork

Clients may voluntarily upload reference images, sketches, or other visual materials as part of a tattoo inquiry.

These materials are used to understand the requested tattoo concept and communicate about a potential project.

We will not publicly publish customer-submitted reference materials or identifiable client photographs for promotional purposes without appropriate permission.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through general inquiry forms.

Tattoo services are subject to applicable age, identification, and consent requirements.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, website technologies, business practices, or applicable legal obligations.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact us.
